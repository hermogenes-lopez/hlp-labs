Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026

This policy covers the Atlassian Marketplace apps published by HLP Labs ("we", "us"), currently Checklists and Merge. It explains what data the apps handle and where that data lives.

Summary

Our apps run entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. They do not send your data to any server we operate or to any third party. We do not see, collect, sell or share your data.

What the apps access

The apps read and write only the Jira data needed to do their job, using the permissions shown when you install them:

  • Checklists reads issues and stores each issue's checklist as a Jira issue property on that issue.
  • Merge reads the two issues being merged (including comments, watchers, attachments and request details) and copies them from one issue to the other. It saves a merge history record in Forge app storage. The record contains only the issue keys, the time and the step results; it holds no names or account IDs.

Where data is stored

All data stays inside your Atlassian cloud site, either in Jira itself or in Forge storage, which Atlassian hosts. It is subject to Atlassian's privacy policy and data residency settings. The apps make no outbound network calls outside Atlassian ("Runs on Atlassian").

What we collect

We do not collect personal data through the apps. We don't use analytics, tracking or cookies in them. Atlassian gives Marketplace partners licensing and installation information, such as site URL, license type and contact details for paid licenses. We use it only to manage licenses and provide support.

If you email us for support, we keep your email and what you send us for as long as we need it to help you.

Retention and deletion

Data the apps store is removed according to Atlassian's Forge data lifecycle after you uninstall the app. You can also delete checklist data by removing the checklist items, and merge history by contacting us. To ask about or delete any support correspondence, email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data. Because the apps' data lives in your Atlassian site, most requests are handled by your site administrator or Atlassian. For anything we hold, such as support emails, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date.
