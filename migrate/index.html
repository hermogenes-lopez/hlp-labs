Why now

Three facts from Atlassian's own pages. Sources are at the bottom, checked 23 September 2026.

  • Connect apps pay Atlassian 20% today and 25% from 1 October 2026.1

    The Connect share went from 15% to 20% on 1 April 2026 and rises to 25% on 1 October 2026. The Forge standard rate is 16%, rising to 17% the same day.

  • Migrated Forge apps pay 0% on the first $1M of lifetime Forge revenue.2

    Since 1 January 2026, partners keep 100% of Forge revenue until they reach $1M lifetime across all their Forge apps. A migrated app qualifies once it is Forge-only: no Connect modules, OAuth, Forge UI. Revenue earned on Connect does not count against the $1M.

  • Connect end of support begins on 31 January 2027.3

    Atlassian moved the date from December 2026 to 31 January 2027. Since March 2026 the Marketplace no longer accepts updates to apps with a Connect descriptor. After end of support, Atlassian only addresses critical security vulnerabilities in Connect.

Both dates are set. The only open number is how many months of share you pay before switching.
